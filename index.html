3D Part Decomposition

Splitting a shape into parts, one click per part

A frozen shape VAE supplies the geometry. A small trainable head turns a single surface point per part into a clean partition of the object’s interior — no part templates, no fixed vocabulary, and no change to the backbone.

Overfitting experiment

Thirteen assets, 16k steps, and one prompt point per part at inference — generalisation deliberately taken out of the picture, so what is left is how well the head can represent a decomposition at all.

Backbone: the TripoSG VAE architecture with the HoloPart released weights, frozen throughout.
